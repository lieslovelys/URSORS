# URS SORS — Simple User Guide

## Students and teachers

### 1. Create an account

1. Open **Register**.
2. Enter your first name, last name, email, role, username, and password.
3. Choose **Send for approval**.
4. Wait for your school admin to approve it. Do not register again.

There is no automatic email message yet. Ask your school admin if you are
still waiting. After approval, open **Log in** and enter your username/password.
Choose **Show** if you want to check the password you typed.

### 2. Find your subject

1. On Home, choose **Browse Subjects**.
2. Choose **Civil Engineering** or **Computer Engineering**.
3. Choose a subject group, then your subject.
4. Choose **General** or **Professor Only**.

**General:** People can share when posting is allowed for their account.
**Professor Only:** Teachers and admins share posts. Students can read and comment.

The links above the page title show where you are. Choose one to go back.
If there are many subjects, use **Find a subject in this group**.

### 3. Share something

1. Find **Create Post** in the subject feed.
2. Write your message.
3. If needed, choose **Attach a file (photo, PDF, etc.)**.
4. Choose **Post (I-share)**.
5. Look for **Your post was shared. Thank you!**

Each file must be 10 MB or smaller. Photos, PDF, Word, Excel, text, ZIP and RAR
are supported. Share only safe files you have permission to share. Never post
someone's private information or passwords.

If you see **Posting is turned off for your account**, contact an admin. You
can still read and comment. Students cannot create Professor Only posts.

### 4. Read, reply, and like

- Choose **Download attached file** to save a shared file.
- Choose **Comments — Read or reply** to see the discussion.
- Write a comment and choose **Send comment**.
- Choose **Like** to react. Choose **Unlike** to remove your reaction.
- Choose **Open post and replies** to keep a link to one discussion.
- To remove your comment, choose **Remove comment**, then **Yes, continue**.
  Choose **Cancel** if you change your mind.

### 5. Find your own posts and updates

- **Home → See My Posts:** See the posts you shared.
- **Notifications:** Blue items are unread. Choose an item to open its post or
  related page. Choose **Mark all as read** when you are done.
- **Ranking:** Counts posts shared. It is **not grades**. Archived posts do not count.
- **Profile:** See your name/role or use the link to find another person.

Older notifications may not have a link to one exact post. They open a related
page instead. An archived or unavailable post may show a friendly explanation.

### 6. Change your photos

1. Open **Profile**.
2. Under **Change profile photo** or **Change cover photo**, choose a photo.
3. Check the preview.
4. Choose **Save profile photo** or **Save cover photo**.

Use JPG, PNG, GIF, or WebP, up to 10 MB. You can only change your own photos.

### On your phone

Choose **Menu** at the top to open navigation. Choose **Close menu** to hide it.
Buttons and cards work the same way as on a computer. Use **Help** whenever you
are unsure. The Home Quick Guide can be hidden with **Got it. Hide this guide.**

### Before you leave

Choose **Logout**, especially on a shared phone or school computer.

## Administrators

### Start on Admin

The Admin page has five tasks: **Pending Accounts**, **All Accounts**,
**Create Account**, **Archived Posts**, and **Ranking**. The counts help you
see what is waiting. Use Home if you want the regular subject-sharing options.

### Approve an account

1. Open **Pending Accounts**.
2. Check the person's name, email, and role. Search if needed.
3. Choose **Approve** next to their name.
4. Read the confirmation banner.

The person can now log in. Approval does not send an email, so tell them outside
the site if needed. If their details are not acceptable, choose **Reject**, then
confirm. They cannot log in. Their account stays unapproved and remains in the
review list; it is not erased. You can approve it later after checking details.

### Allow or stop posting

1. Open **All Accounts**.
2. Search by name, username, or email.
3. Choose **Stop posting** or **Allow posting**, then confirm.
4. Read the result banner.

Stopping posting does not delete existing posts. The person can still read and
comment. An account must still be approved before the person can log in.

### Create an account yourself

Open **Create Account**, fill in the fields, and choose **Create account**.
This account is immediately approved. Give the person their username and
password privately. Do not send passwords in a public post.

### Archive or restore a post

1. On a post, open **Manage this post**.
2. Choose **Archive post**, then **Yes, continue**.
3. To bring it back, open **Archived Posts** and choose **Restore post**.

Teachers can also archive and restore. Admin **Remove post** also puts a post
in the archive. Nothing is permanently erased by these buttons. Comment
removal hides the comment but keeps the stored row.

### Common questions

- **Someone cannot log in:** Check approval first. Then ask them to check their
  username, password, and capital letters. There is no self-service password
  reset screen yet; contact the system maintainer for password changes.
- **Someone cannot post:** Check their posting setting and whether they are
  trying to post in Professor Only as a student.
- **A form expired:** Go back, refresh, and submit again. Do not keep retrying
  an old form after logging out or switching accounts.
- **A page is missing:** Choose **Go back Home**, then open Subjects again.
- **Testing accounts appear:** Development checks keep labelled `ux_...` and
  `smoke_...` accounts rather than deleting data. Leave them unapproved.
