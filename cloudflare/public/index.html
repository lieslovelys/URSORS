How can we help?

A quick guide to learning and sharing.

I can't log in

Check your username and password. New accounts need school administrator approval. Contact your administrator if you are still waiting. Original PHP accounts do not automatically work in this preview.

How do I share a file?

Upload it to your preferred file service, configure its sharing permissions, and paste the HTTPS link into a subject post. Direct uploads are not supported here.

General or Professor Only?

Everyone can read both feeds. Only teachers and administrators can publish in Professor Only.

Admin tools

Home shows community posts. Pending Accounts lists registrations awaiting approval; All Accounts manages approval and posting permissions. Admin opens the management overview. Create Account adds an already approved student, teacher, or administrator. Archived Posts lets teachers and admins restore resources.

Notifications and discussions

Notifications show account updates and activity on your posts. Only you can mark your notifications read. You can remove your own comments; admins can also moderate comments. Removal hides the stored comment instead of deleting it permanently.

About this version

This is a local Cloudflare preview. Password recovery, additional profile fields, and migration of existing PHP accounts and files are not available yet. It has not been deployed as a public website.
